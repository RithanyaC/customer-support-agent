Return Policy

- Items may be returned within 30 days of delivery.
- Footwear, apparel, and home goods are eligible if they are unused, in original packaging, and not damaged.
- Final sale items, personalized goods, and opened consumables are non-returnable.
- Return requests must be approved before a return label is issued.
- If the item is not eligible, the customer receives a clear rejection reason.

Refund Policy

- Approved refunds are processed within 5 to 10 business days after return inspection.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method.
- Customers may receive a store credit when the original payment method is unavailable or the return is outside policy.
- Refund status can be tracked after the return is approved and processed.

Exchange Policy

- Exchanges are allowed for the same item or an equivalent item of equal value within 30 days.
- Exchange requests require a matching product available in stock.
- Exchanges cannot be processed for final-sale items or personalized products.

Warranty Policy

- Defective items are covered by a 1-year limited warranty for manufacturing defects.
- Normal wear and tear, misuse, or accidental damage are not covered.
- Warranty claims require proof of purchase and the product serial number or order number.

FAQ

- Where is my order? Customers can use their order number and customer account to verify order status.
- How long does a refund take? Refunds typically appear within 5 to 10 business days after approval.
- Can I return a product if it was delivered last week? Yes, if it is in saleable condition and within 30 days of delivery.
- Do you offer exchanges? Yes, subject to product availability and policy constraints.
